# OFFWARD_PRODUCT_CANON

## Status
Locked.

## Core Identity
Offward is a travel/adventure platform for places we actually explore, document, and sometimes offer people a chance to join.

## Canonical Product Definition
**Offward is a living travel platform built around places we actually explore, routes we test, stories we tell, and selected experiences people can join.**

## Current Functions
Offward may:
- publish places
- publish routes
- publish stories
- publish videos
- publish expeditions and events
- offer selected tours
- accept enquiries and direct contact

## What Offward Is Not
Offward is not:
- an online travel agency
- a marketplace
- an ecommerce store
- a mass-tour booking platform
- a generic scraped travel directory

## Current Country States

### Bosnia
- biker-focused tours only for now
- bookings/enquiries open
- no broader Bosnia tour products are advertised yet

### Ireland
- active exploration and content
- places, routes, stories, and videos may be published
- no formal product is required

### Spain
- upcoming expedition
- expedition content may include routes, places, stories, videos, and partner/sponsor material

## Commercial Rules
- no public prices for now
- no online checkout
- no online card payments
- tours are arranged through direct enquiry/contact
- payment is handled on site

## Content Principle
Offward should primarily publish places, routes, stories, videos, and experiences that we actually visit, explore, test, document, or deliberately feature.

## Brand Principle
Offward should feel like:
- travel
- exploration
- reporting
- local experiences
- real places and real routes

Offward should not feel like:
- a generic holiday-package site
- a mass-tourism catalogue
- a fake marketplace full of empty products

## Current Audience
Offward is for people who want to:
- discover interesting places
- follow expeditions
- explore routes
- watch travel content
- read stories and reports
- potentially join selected tours

## Future-Proofing
The product architecture must support future:
- countries
- activities
- tours
- expeditions
- partners and sponsors
- richer maps
- API-backed content

These additions must not require changing the core product definition.

## Locked Principle
Countries are not products.

A country is a content and exploration hub. Tours, routes, places, stories, videos, and expeditions may exist inside a country independently.
